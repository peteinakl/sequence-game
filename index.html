How to Play Sequence

🎯 Objective

Form lines of 5 tokens horizontally, vertically, or diagonally on the 10x10 card board. Complete the required number of sequences (1 or 2, toggleable above) before the machine does!

⭐ Wild Corner Spaces

The 4 corner spaces (marked ★ FREE) are wild for all players. They act as already-placed bonus chips for both you and the machine, so you only need 4 of your own chips in a line connecting to a corner!

🃏 Special Jacks

Two-Eyed Jacks (♣ Clubs & ♦ Diamonds): Completely WILD. Play one to place a chip on any unoccupied space on the entire board.
One-Eyed Jacks (♠ Spades & ♥ Hearts): REMOVAL. Play one to remove any opponent's chip from the board!
Protected Sequences: Once a 5-in-a-row sequence is completed, its chips are locked (glowing gold with ★) and cannot be removed by One-Eyed Jacks.

🔄 Dead Cards

If both spaces on the board matching a card in your hand are already covered by chips, the card becomes DEAD. A dead card will display an orange badge; click it to discard and draw a replacement without losing your turn.

🏆 Victory!

You connected 5 in a row and won the match!
